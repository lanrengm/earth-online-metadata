隐私政策与个人信息保护声明

我们非常重视您的隐私。人生面板（Earth Online）严格遵循「零隐私收集」原则。本隐私政策旨在向您说明本应用在保护您隐私方面的坚定立场和数据处理方式。

Privacy Policy & Info Protection

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. Life Board (Earth Online) strictly adheres to the principle of 'Zero Privacy Collection'. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling policies and guarantees.

1. 零隐私数据收集声明

由于本应用不具备后台服务器与任何数据上传的网络接口，我们不会、也无法收集您的任何个人身份信息（如姓名、邮箱、手机号等）、敏感生物识别数据、位置轨迹信息、设备唯一标识符（IMEI/IDFA等）或应用交互日志。

2. 设备敏感权限请求与使用

为保障本地业务功能的正常使用，本应用可能在特定场景下向您请求设备敏感权限：

  • 存储/媒体库权限：仅在您主动修改头像（从相册中选择本地图片）或者进行「数据备份/恢复」（导出或读取备份压缩包）时申请。该权限读取和写入的数据完全在您本地处理，绝对不含有任何形式的网络传输。

3. 零第三方 SDK 与网络请求声明

我们极为注重应用环境的纯净与安全。本应用中未集成任何第三方的商业广告推送 SDK、用户标识追踪 SDK（如各类分析统计工具等）。唯一发起的主动网络数据获取为：

在您使用「账本汇率换算」功能时，会通过受信任的公共分发网络（如 jsDelivr 与 GitHub）向欧洲央行（European Central Bank）发起公开汇率数据的被动拉取请求。该请求仅限用于下载最新汇率数值，我们绝不会借此上传、收集或共享您的任何记账资产、财务日志或个人数据，所有操作依然完全局限在您本地设备上。

4. 数据的彻底物理销毁

您对您的本地数据拥有绝对控制权。除了在应用内进行修改和删除，您随时可以通过系统自带的「清除应用数据」或「卸载应用」功能，在本地物理介质上一次性彻底销毁本应用所创建的全部本地数据库与配置文件，实现无可挽回的绝对安全删除。

5. 政策修订与支持联络

为符合应用商店最新的合规标准，本隐私政策可能会适时进行更新，最新版本将在本页面展示。

当您使用「意见反馈」功能向我们发送邮件时，我们会附带软件版本及平台环境信息，以便于为您排查和定位问题，这些数据仅用于处理您的反馈，不会用于任何其它目的。如果您对本隐私政策或本地数据处理方式有任何问题或宝贵意见，随时可以通过开发支持渠道联络我们。

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Since this application does not operate any remote servers or database backends, we do not, and cannot, collect, upload, transmit, or share any personal identity information (e.g., name, phone number, email), biometric data, GPS coordinates, device unique identifiers (IMEI/IDFA), or usage telemetry.

2. Request and Usage of Permissions

To support essential local logic, this application may request permission prompts in limited scenarios:

  • Storage / Media Access: Only requested when you explicitly choose to edit your profile avatar (selecting an image from your local gallery) or use the 'Backup & Restore' feature (exporting or importing zip files). All read/write operations occur purely offline.

3. Zero Third-Party SDKs & Network Fetching Statement

We maintain a clean and trace-free environment. This application does not embed any advertising networks or user identity analytics trackers. To support the bookkeeping multi-currency rate conversion feature:

The app may passively fetch public exchange rate data published by the European Central Bank (ECB) via trusted public CDN networks (such as jsDelivr and GitHub). This request is solely used to download raw numeric rate ratios for local conversion. We strictly guarantee that no financial ledgers, transactional logs, or personal metrics are ever uploaded, recorded, or shared during this query. All operations remain strictly 100% offline on your local device.

4. Permanent Physical Deletion of Data

You enjoy full sovereignty over your database. Beyond in-app CRUD deletions, you can instantly and physically wipe all local configurations and database files from your flash storage by using the system settings: 'Clear App Data' or by uninstalling the application entirely.

5. Changes to This Policy & Support Contact

This policy may be updated periodically to remain compliant with application store standards, with revisions published inside the app.

When you use the "Feedback" feature to send us an email, we will include the software version and platform environment information to help troubleshoot and locate the issue, which is solely used to address your feedback. For any questions, inquiries, or suggestions regarding this privacy framework, feel free to contact us through development channels.
